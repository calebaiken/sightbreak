# Art direction

SIGHTBREAK is an original near-future tactical shooter set in working logistics sites. The Relay Yard transfer station is the first. The visual goal is the clarity and material honesty associated with Counter-Strike 2's presentation (see `VISUAL_GAP_ANALYSIS.md` for what that comparison does and does not rest on), in our own identity. It is never a copy.

## Pillars

1. **Readable first.**
   - Silhouettes, team colours and lighting are judged at gameplay distance on a phone before they are judged up close.
   - Detail that hurts target reads is removed, whatever it costs.
2. **Honest materials.**
   - Every surface is a physically based material: anodised aluminium, parkerised steel, glass-filled polymer, rubber, painted steel, concrete.
   - Wear follows use: edges, grip points and walking lines, not uniform noise.
3. **Engineered, not decorated.**
   - Objects look manufactured, with real dimensions, fasteners, parting lines and draft angles.
   - Surface interest comes from construction, not greebles.
4. **Restrained palette, purposeful colour.**
   - The world sits in desaturated industrial blue-greys and warm concrete.
   - Saturated colour is reserved for team identity, objectives, signage and cosmetic finishes.

## Weapons

- Real-scale forms with a distinct SIGHTBREAK identity.
- **Relay AR:** a skeletonised stock with a cheek riser, an angular reflex sight, a rounded-hexagon slotted handguard and a three-port brake.
- **Default finish:** two-tone. Dark anodised receivers carry the cosmetic finish; earth-tone polymer furniture keeps the weapon readable when the finish is dark.
- **Cosmetic finishes** are surface treatments over the baked occlusion and normals. They never change the silhouette, so a finish can never affect visibility or hitboxes.

## Characters (planned)

Operators wear practical layered kit. The two sides read by silhouette features (helmet profile, pack shape) and a team colour band. Faces stay covered or in shadow, so no real person's likeness is ever implied.

## Environment (planned)

Concrete, corrugated steel, crane steel and container stacks, with signage in the existing SIGHTBREAK typographic system. Lighting is late-afternoon sun with cool sky fill: sunlit lanes read warm and shaded cover reads cool, with enough ambient that a player in shade stays visible.
