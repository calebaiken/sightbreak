## Security review

Positive boundaries inspected:

- Both welcome and recurring wire snapshots pass a server-bound recipient actor ID.
- Public roster retains names/team/alive/stats; enemy private combat state is never emitted.
- Unknown enemy position is absent on the wire, with geometry/collision/private cached state revoked in the honest client.
- Server keeps full actors and hit detection independent of filtering.
- Teammate health/funds and own inventory/ammunition permissions are explicit allowlists.
- Objective carrier/current interactions and hidden dropped location are scoped; carrier-drop/recovery announcements are team-only.
- Shot and impact channels are separately authorized; hidden shots expose quantized audible origin/weapon, no hidden actorID.
- Silent smoke uses spatial relevance instead of fictitious sound; explosions retain existing audio entitlement.
- Dead observers use permitted living teammate and collision-clamped camera; eliminated team has no enemy viewpoint.
- Actor/team/reconnect permission is recomputed without a continuing visibility cache.
- Protocol increments to3 and preserves pre-gameplay raw auth; schema checks before client mutation and non-object deserialization bounds remain.

Residual risks are material and documented:

-0.40m sample padding, up-to0.65m observer advance,360-degree relevance and previous observations provide modified clients limited extra information. Finite rays are not a complete PVS; new geometry/narrow apertures need additional fixtures.
- Only confidently opaque central smoke is withheld. Growth/fade/outer lobes and uncertain rims remain disclosed. This intentionally avoids making visible players invisible; it is not complete smoke-wallhack prevention.
- Audible gunfire can reveal approximate location/weapon over time. Utility visual bounds disclose a conservative visible region. Neither is perfect information secrecy.
- Authoritative/client spectator positions differ transiently until movement smoothing/reconciliation is implemented and measured. No mobile latency/loss acceptance is established here.
- Server admission resources, insecure Internet transport, local movement responsiveness, durable rewards and long-duration soak are outside this phase's acceptance.
