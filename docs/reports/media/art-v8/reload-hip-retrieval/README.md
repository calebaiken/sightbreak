# The reload, round 3: a source for the replacement, handling, sounds, grip, flash

Branch `claude/cs2-fidelity-art-production`, from `fceefa1` (art-v7). **Who has judged this:** my own measurements, and one read-only review by an agent that shares my model family (below). **Nobody has looked at it on a phone, nobody has listened to the sounds, and no human has judged any of it.** Every timing and cost is from Mesa llvmpipe under Xvfb with the Dummy audio driver. Tests prove what they measure; none of them proves it looks or sounds right. **The independent review scored every category below the owner's 8.5 target (5.5 to 6.5), and the fixes made after it did not change that: I have not re-scored, and I do not claim the target is met.**

## What changed

1. **Where the replacement comes from.** After the old magazine is let go (0.37) the empty hand goes down and to the left to a magazine pouch at the hip, **below the bottom of the frame**; the sleeve swings out and up (a small elbow bias) so it runs down the left edge. The hand takes the replacement there (0.50), the replacement is **first drawn at that instant, in the hand, with 3 % of it inside the frame** (a test), and the hand brings it up, across and under the well. The hand is out of the picture from 0.41 to 0.60 of the reload (19 %, 0.43 s of 2.25 s); the pouch itself is not drawn. This is the brief's "carefully staged off-screen retrieval", not a visible pouch. **The sleeve is itself out of the picture in 1 of the 10 hip poses** (it was kept in in the first version of this round at the cost of a larger elbow bias, which the review called unnatural; it was reduced).
2. **Handling.** Every path through the clip's keys is a Catmull-Rom spline with continuous velocity and `hold` keys only at a grasp, a let-go and the seat (the v7 motion eased between keys and read as linear). The replacement rocks about its front lip (8.8° as it enters, square at the stop), the seat gives the rifle a short kick (a quarter of it with reduced motion), the swing-out is a smooth arc, and on the return the fingers keep a partial curl until they are clear of the handguard instead of opening flat. Peak speeds: magazine 1.82 m/s, hand 2.80 m/s; peak accelerations on the clip's own 60 Hz grid: magazine 17.0 m/s², hand 41.5 m/s² (bounds in `weapon_art_test.gd`: 2.0 and 3.0 m/s, 20 and 50 m/s²).
3. **Hands.** Extensor tendons on the back of each hand; the support-hand grasp re-tuned (roll 24.5°) so the pinky and middle fingertip stay in view; the gloves are a cooler graphite so they read as a different material from the olive sleeve. The contact review (Blender, every frame): left arm 0.0 mm into the rifle and 0.9 mm into the magazine, right arm 1.47 mm into the trigger (the trigger finger rests on it by design). **The hands are not anatomically convincing yet**: see "Not done".
4. **Sounds.** Six events of the presentation clock (unlatch, extract, pouch, land, enter, seat), each once per reload, on four separate voices of their own (`reload_audio.gd`), twelve synthesized sounds (two variants of each, alternating by reload). A cancelled reload stops what is playing and plays nothing more; weapon change, round reset and death do the same. The shot has its own player.
5. **Grip and materials.** The pistol grip is its own mesh (one more draw), with a palm swell, a flared base, three finger grooves and a recessed stippled panel on each side, in a graphite material that is darker again in the game (0.3 of its baked albedo, no fill floor). The stipple is raised dots, not the relief that read as rock. The arms got a readability floor and a rim light.
6. **Muzzle flash.** The thin straight spikes that drew the flash as a star are gone. After the review the flash was rebuilt a second time: two short lathed cores and three soft lobes with a glow disc (280 triangles, one node), a 40 ms light that exists only in first person, and a puff of smoke that thins out over 0.45 s. At gameplay size it now reads as a soft bright blob with faint petals; I have not judged it against real footage, and in a close-up the lobes are still visibly geometric.
7. **Reduced motion.** Three variants of the rifle's roll and lift, compared in `../reduced-motion/README.md`. **The default is unchanged and the choice is open.**

Glove / sleeve: 0.79 sun, 0.90 backlight, 0.82 shade, 0.89 dark. Grip / glove: 0.57, 0.72, 0.85, 0.75. Against the round before (`logs/light-probe-before-this-round.txt`) the gloves in the dark interior went from 15.5 to 20 (the target was 20). **The glove-to-sleeve gap did not improve** (the arms' fill floor lifts both): the cooler glove colour separates them by hue, not by value, and in shade and the dark the review still found them hard to tell apart. ## Defects found while doing this (and not hidden)

- A 11.6 mm "penetration" of a hand that had not moved: a BVH rebuilt every frame picked a different nearest face once in 61 frames (fixed: one BVH in the magazine's own space). Then a 17 mm "penetration" a fingertip beside the handguard: the nearest-face sign is wrong at a convex edge (fixed: the sign is confirmed by ray parity).
- After the rifle's rebuild three visibility rules failed: the pinky dipped to 90 px at one pose, the middle fingertip to 77 px at another, and the hand on the rise was 5,575 px. I retuned the grasp (roll 25 to 24.5°) and the carry and elbow paths until they passed on a 0.01 grid. **That is tuning to the measure.** The margins are thin: while a magazine is held the index fingertip is never visible (0 px in every such pose), so the rule rests on the middle, ring and pinky tips, whose least counts are 102 px (middle, at 0.36) and 115 px (pinky, at 0.32) against a floor of 100.
- A fill floor I first tried for the dark interior made the arms a flat grey with no glove, sleeve or form (seen in a frame, not in the numbers: the numbers looked fine). Retuned until the frame looked right.
- The grip's baked graphite had the same median luma as the gloves; only the probe found it.
- The visibility test did not quit when a `--csv` path was relative (a failed write left it running for 30 minutes); fixed.
- A first redesign of the flash exceeded the triangle budget (312 against 300); the main lathes were reduced to 10 sides.
- The studio-lighting test ("at most three lights") failed when the flash light existed in every build; the light now exists only in first person.
- **Five of the 30 negative controls of the first run were missed.** Three named a test that cannot see that defect (wrong lists), one was redundant (two independent cancel paths), and one was a real gap: the cancel capture cancelled when every sound was almost over, so a build that never stopped its voices passed. Fixed (the cancel now comes 90 ms into the landing, whose second bounce is 190 ms after it starts; the sound's second bounce was moved later for it). `logs/negative-controls-run1.txt` is that run.

## Controls

Final run on `bd6db8a` in a clean worktree: `logs/negative-controls.txt`.

Baseline (no defect injected): all eight test runs pass. **29 defects injected one at a time, 29 caught** (every listed test fails cleanly on each, with a summary line and not a crash), 0 missed, 0 crashed. The list (`logs/negative-controls.txt` has each one's failing tests and messages): N1 the replacement is the first magazine again (one node, not two); N2 the replacement is never drawn; N3 the nodes do not swap when the reload ends; N4 the magazine is held 30 mm from where the hand grips it; N5 the old magazine comes back to the well while the replacement is drawn; N6 reduced motion shrinks the reload staging to a fifth; N7 the clicks follow the authoritative fraction instead of the visible reload; N8 the seat click fires 0.05 of the reload early; N9 the seat click fires at the let-go instead of the seat; N10 a clock correction that goes back over events plays them twice; N11 the seat click plays the release sound; N12 brass lands at the shooter's feet again; N13 the animation completes the reload by writing the timer; N19 the SMG plays the rigged hand's whole set of sounds; N20 reduced motion no longer calms the procedural weapons' reload dip; N21 a reload restarted with no idle frame between does not reset its events; N22 the replacement is drawn from the start of the hip trip: it floats in view with no hand on it; N14 the seat phase is changed in reload_phases.gd without rebuilding the clip; A1 a reload cut short leaves its sounds playing; A2 every reload sound uses the same voice, so each cuts the one before; A4 the slide-out has no sound; A5 the reload sounds play 80 dB down (the voices are muted); A6 a cancelled reload does not stop its voices (real audio, a cancel as the landing starts); N15 a hand that does not reach the magazine (knuckle 50 mm off); N16 the previous grasp, which hides the thumb behind the magazine; N17 the previous approach from below the frame (the hand leaves the picture); N23 the replacement taken from just under the well, in view: it appears in the picture before the hand has it; N24 the magazine and hand move in straight lines between keys (robotic, with velocity jumps); N18 the replacement fetched from far below the frame. Each is a defect I thought of; **defects nobody thought to inject are not covered, and a control is only as good as the test list I named for it** (the first run missed 5 of 30 for exactly that reason, fixed above). The harness restores every file it touches; this run was in a separate worktree at `bd6db8a`.

## Not done, and what I would do next, in order

1. **The hands are still not convincing.** The support hand reads as a glove around a stick, the grip hand as three blobs on the grip; there are tendons now and nothing else new. At gameplay size they read; at close size (see `frames/`) the fingers are sausages without joint definition. Real work here is the finger geometry and a stronger glove detail pass in `relay_ar_arms.py`, and it changes every number above, so it needs the whole loop again (about 5 minutes of Blender build per attempt plus the tests).
2. A visible pouch, or at least a visible sleeve for the whole hip trip (the arm is out of the picture in 1 of 10 hip poses by default, 9 of 10 in `calm` and 8 of 10 in `still`).
3. The glove-to-sleeve value gap in shade and in the dark, and the dark grip against the dark rifle.
4. The rifle goes pale and beige around ticks 120 to 134 of the reload in sunlit views, which the review reported and I see in `sheets/sunlit.jpg`; I did not find the cause.
5. A listening review of the sounds, a human review of the sequences, and any look at all on a phone. The sounds are my synthesis, not a design anyone has judged.
6. A decision on reduced motion (`../reduced-motion/README.md`).
7. Competitive sightlines: the crosshair box is clear, but the rifle covers 7 to 8 % of the frame during a reload and lifts and rolls as before; I have not measured the sightline to targets a player would watch for.
