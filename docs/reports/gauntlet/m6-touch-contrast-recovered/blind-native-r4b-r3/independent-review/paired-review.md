# Blind review of 32 native touch-control pairs

The strongest visible treatment gives minimum-scale labels enough space to stay inside their circles and separates the mobile grenade controls. I prefer that treatment in the 24 minimum-scale or mobile maximum-scale pairs. The eight desktop maximum-scale pairs are ties: the badge and stroke changes are visible, but both choices remain readable and have essentially the same hierarchy and spacing.

A/B labels change between scenes, so one overall letter would be misleading. My per-scene choices below are the result; I do not know the candidate/source mapping. These are visual preferences, not a pass on numeric gates, implementation correctness or interaction behavior.

## Overall findings and limits

The minimum-scale improvement is the clearest result. Labels visibly crossing a circular button's border make the icon, label and boundary harder to parse as one object. Roomier circles fix much of that without covering a large additional part of the play area. On mobile maximum scale, separating the two grenade circles and moving USE away from their junction removes an obvious crowding problem. The desktop maximum-scale differences provide no comparable win in this packet.

The largest remaining visual gap is minimum-scale internal padding. RELOAD still nearly spans its circle, and grenade glyphs sit close to quantity badges. More control diameter alone does not settle typography and icon/count spacing.

Across both choices, the large FIRE circle establishes the primary action, the center remains mostly open, and the dark filled controls survive the supplied white, gray, smoke and arena backgrounds. The dim FRAG 0 versus brighter SMOKE 1 gives a visible unavailable/available distinction. Red fill plus a gold rim distinguishes the supplied held captures from idle; that says nothing about when or whether a real hold is delivered. The always-visible thin red FIRE arc in idle remains semantically ambiguous from screenshots alone. No selected inventory state or full live-HUD integration is demonstrated.

I found no visible source captions or mismatched scene/state that invalidate these pair decisions. All 32 combinations supplied by the root were reviewed, so I did not choose only favorable backgrounds. I cannot independently certify how the packet was sampled or whether omitted states, custom layouts, other viewports or live gameplay would reverse a finding. Flat gray/white and a repeated smoke fixture test contrast, not the full range of dynamic backgrounds.

The supplied references informed hierarchy, separation and contrast criteria. This packet is a touch-control fixture with no basis for a CS2 parity or gunfeel claim. Native byte integrity also does not establish source correctness or ownership. Numeric gates, regressions, interaction routing, latency, release behavior, multi-touch, physical reach, safe areas, live/custom HUDs and navigation require their own evidence. None of these visual preferences overrides those checks.
