Principles reported by press coverage of Valve's announcement (2023) and later patch notes:
- Upgrade maps: Source 2 lighting with a physically based rendering system: "realistic materials, lighting, and reflections" (80.lv, Gematsu, HotHardware).
- Map tiers: touchstone (lighting + character read only), upgrade (new lighting physics + textures, e.g. Nuke), full overhaul (Overpass rebuilt: textures, lighting, structures).
- Readability is actively tuned after launch: patch notes "remove or tweak models and textures to improve player readability", "adjust lighting in certain areas to improve player readability" (DSOGaming Oct 25 patch).
- Smokes: "dynamic volumetric objects that interact with the environment, and react to lighting, gunfire, and explosions"; bullets/HE briefly clear; fills spaces, travels through doors; lit by unified lighting (team tint) (PCGamesN, esports.gg, press-start).
- Effects and skins re-done on Source 2 lighting/particles.
